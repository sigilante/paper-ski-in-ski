\section{Addendum: self-reference above level 0}\label{app:ifix}

The open problem \emph{Quotation above level 0} (\S\ref{sec:open}) asks whether
re-encoding, $\langle t\rangle \mapsto \langle\langle t\rangle\rangle$, recovers the
self-quotation of \citet{Jay2016,Jay2017}.  It recovers self-\emph{reference}, and
it cannot recover self-\emph{quotation}.

Write $R$ for the re-encoder, with $R\,\langle t\rangle \twoheadrightarrow
\langle\langle t\rangle\rangle$ for every $t$, and $\mathit{App}$ for the object
type's application constructor, so that $\langle f\,a\rangle = \mathit{App}\,
\langle f\rangle\,\langle a\rangle$.

\begin{proposition}[Intensional fixed points]\label{prop:ifix}
For every closed term $g$ over $\{S,K,I\}$ there is a closed term $t$ with
$t \twoheadrightarrow g\,\langle t\rangle$.  In particular ($g = I$) there is a
closed term $t$ with $t \twoheadrightarrow \langle t\rangle$: a quine.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Let $h_g = \lambda y.\, g\,(\mathit{App}\;y\;(R\,y))$, compiled by bracket
abstraction, and $t = h_g\,\langle h_g\rangle$.  Then
\[
t \;\twoheadrightarrow\; g\,(\mathit{App}\,\langle h_g\rangle\,(R\,\langle h_g\rangle))
  \;\twoheadrightarrow\; g\,(\mathit{App}\,\langle h_g\rangle\,\langle\langle h_g\rangle\rangle)
  \;=\; g\,\langle h_g\,\langle h_g\rangle\rangle \;=\; g\,\langle t\rangle .
\qedhere\]
\end{proof}

This is Kleene's second recursion theorem for the calculus, and in the
terms of \citet{Kavvos2017} it is the intensional fixed-point rule: from any
$g$ that consumes code, a term that receives its own code.  It does not
contradict Proposition~\ref{prop:quote}.  The only quotation is
$\langle h_g\rangle$, the code of a closed term fixed before anything runs (a
level-0 name in the sense of \S\ref{sec:site}); every later step is $R$ or
$\mathit{App}$ acting on data.  No step takes a live term to its code, and none
can.  So the open problem divides.  Self-reference is available from one
compile-time quotation plus re-encoding.  Self-quotation, a closed $Q$ with
$Q\,M \twoheadrightarrow \langle M\rangle$, stays excluded at every level.

Proposition~\ref{prop:ifix} is mechanized in the \texttt{lean-ski} repository
(\url{https://github.com/sigilante/lean-ski}, \texttt{Ski/Ifix.lean}, commit
\texttt{6c92722}) as \texttt{Ifix.ifix} and \texttt{Ifix.quine}, over that
repository's Scott encoding (the four-continuation encoding of
\S\ref{sec:construction}) and its proved re-encoder \texttt{Requote.requote}.
Both depend only on the axioms \texttt{propext} and \texttt{Quot.sound} and are
in the repository's axiom gate.  An independent check in Python, with this
paper's encoding, compiles the quine to $69{,}268$ atoms ($|h| = 1{,}788$,
$|\langle h\rangle| = 67{,}480$) and needs $2{,}855{,}152$ contractions to read
$\langle t\rangle$ back from $t$.  The $38\times$ growth from $h$ to
$\langle h\rangle$ is the cost of the $\mathit{App}$ constructor at every node,
and it compounds per level as the tower costs of \S\ref{sec:tower} do.
